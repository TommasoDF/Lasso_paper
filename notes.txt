- What are the effect of agents using data uncorrelated with fundamentals to forecast returns?

- This paper
- Offers a rationale as to why agents might behave in this way (cross-moment consistent equilibrium)
- Analyzes different types of forecasting rules simple OLS, WLS with constant gain, LASSO regression

- Main finding: memory (gain) is crucial
- With decreasing gains (OLS) only cross-moment consistent equilibrium is REE 
- With constant gain (WLS) CMCE mean coincides with REE but distribution is different (excess volatilty)
- Lasso does not change the result
- We theoretically quanitify probability of window of predictabilities (returns being predictable because agents believe so based on their statistical tool)
